Photograph a receipt. We read it, work out what you bought, and pay you points for shopping with businesses that behave well — and more for telling us what was in the basket.

How it works

1. Scan

Take a photo of a till receipt. No typing, no barcodes.

2. Confirm

Receipts abbreviate things beyond recognition. Occasionally we will ask you which product a line meant — and your answer teaches the system, so nobody is asked about that one again.

3. Rate and earn

Rate what you bought against ethical criteria the membership sets. Points follow the ratings, so the better the businesses you use, the more you earn.

What happens to your receipt

The photograph is deleted within 24 hours of being read — usually within seconds. What you bought stays attached to you for 30 days so you can rate it, and is then permanently anonymised.
